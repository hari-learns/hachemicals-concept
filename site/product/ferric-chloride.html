Ferric Chloride — Coagulant & Oxidizer for Water Treatment and Industrial Use

Supplied by HA International Chemicals Trading LLC, a chemical supplier based in the UAE. Ferric Chloride is a powerful inorganic compound widely used in water and wastewater treatment, PCB etching, and various industrial chemical processes. Known for its strong coagulating and oxidizing properties, it’s a reliable and efficient solution for removing impurities and heavy metals.

Key Benefits

  • Effective Coagulant — Rapidly removes suspended solids and organic matter in water treatment processes.
  • Powerful Oxidizer — Efficiently breaks down contaminants and pollutants.
  • High Solubility — Available in both anhydrous and liquid forms for easy handling and application.
  • High Purity — Industrial-grade and laboratory-grade options available.

    Key Applications

    • Water & Wastewater Treatment — Removes impurities, suspended solids, and heavy metals.
    • Industrial Effluent Treatment — Treats process water before discharge or reuse.
    • Metal Surface Cleaning — Used in surface preparation and cleaning applications.
    • PCB Etching — Standard etchant for printed circuit board manufacturing.
    • Catalyst Preparation — Used as a component in various industrial chemical processes.

Available Forms & Packaging

Available in anhydrous (industrial-grade) and liquid (industrial or laboratory-grade) forms, packaged in both drums and bulk (ton) quantities to suit different order volumes.
