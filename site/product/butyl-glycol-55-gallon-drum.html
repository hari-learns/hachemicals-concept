Butyl Glycol 55 gallon drum

Butyl Glycol, also known as 2-Butoxyethanol, is a versatile glycol ether solvent widely used in industrial and commercial applications. It offers excellent solvency for both polar and non-polar substances, making it a key ingredient in many formulations.

  • Chemical Name: 2-Butoxyethanol

  • Formula: C₆H₁₄O₂

  • Purity: ≥ 99%

  • 🛠 Applications:

    • Paints, coatings, and varnishes (solvent)

    • Surface cleaners and degreasers

    • Printing inks

    • Industrial detergents and household cleaners

    • Oilfield chemicals

    • Textile and leather processing

  • Appearance: Clear, colorless liquid

  • Odor: Mild ether-like

  • Packaging: 200-liter drum / 20-liter can / custom packaging available
