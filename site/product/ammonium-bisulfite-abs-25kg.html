Ammonium Bisulfite (ABS) 25kg

Ammonium Sulfite (ABS) is an inorganic compound commonly used in the chemical, water treatment, and paper industries. It appears as a white to pale yellow crystalline powder and is known for its strong reducing properties. Our high-purity industrial-grade ABS is suitable for a variety of applications, including oxygen scavenging, photographic processing, and chemical synthesis.

    Ammonium Bisulfite is an inorganic compound that exists as a colourless, pale-yellow solution with a slight sulfur odour. It is formed by the reaction of Ammonia and Sulfurous acid. This seemingly simple compound which is also known as Ammonium Hydrogen Sulfite acts as a powerful oxygen scavenger, a reducing agent and an effective preservative making it a very useful chemical for various applications across a wide range of industries. It is used in water treatment to remove chlorine and other oxidizing agents from water. It helps in neutralising the chlorine ensuring that the water is safe for further industrial use or to be discharged into the environment.
