Cenosphere — Lightweight Additive for Oil & Gas Cementing and Drilling

HA Cenosphere delivers the balance of strength, stability, and cost-effectiveness needed for demanding downhole conditions — supplied by HA International Chemicals Trading LLC, a chemical supplier based in the UAE.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Value / Range
Density 0.70 – 0.85 g/cc
Compressive Strength 3,000 – 4,800 psi
Heat Resistance Up to 1,500°C
Particle Size 5 – 500 microns

Key Benefits

  • Enhanced Cementing Performance — Improves cement strength and zonal isolation while reducing slurry density, resulting in robust wellbore integrity.
  • Weight Reduction in Drilling Fluids — Minimizes lost circulation risk in deepwater and high-pressure wells, cutting down non-productive time.
  • Thermal & Chemical Stability — Maintains integrity in high-temperature environments and resists chemical reactions, extending well lifespan.
  • Cost-Effective & Eco-Friendly — Reduces material usage and operational costs while lowering environmental impact.

Key Applications

  • Cementing — Reduces cement slurry density during well completion, improving pumpability and well integrity.
  • Drilling Fluids — Improves viscosity, reduces fluid loss, and helps control wellbore pressure.
  • Wellbore Strengthening — Reinforces the cement bond and improves fracture resistance to help prevent wellbore collapse.
