Drilling Detergent — Surfactant Additive for Water-Based Drilling Fluids

Supplied by HA International Chemicals Trading LLC, a chemical supplier based in the UAE. Our Drilling Detergent is a specially formulated blend of surfactants designed to reduce surface tension, minimize bit balling, and enhance lubricity in water-based drilling fluids.

Key Benefits

  • Improved Cuttings Removal — Reduces surface tension to help lift and transport drill cuttings more effectively.
  • Reduced Bit Balling — Minimizes clay and cuttings buildup on the drill bit, improving penetration rate.
  • Foam Control — Helps manage foaming for more stable, predictable fluid performance.
  • High-Temperature Stability — Performs reliably even in demanding high-temperature well conditions.

Key Applications

  • Onshore & Offshore Drilling — Suitable for a wide range of well environments.
  • Water-Based Mud Systems — Compatible with most standard mud system formulations.
  • High-Temperature Wells — Maintains performance where thermal stability is critical.

Packaging & Availability

Available in both 25kg drums and bulk (ton) bags to suit different order volumes. Contact us for current stock and pricing.
