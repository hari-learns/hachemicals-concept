Defoamer Supplier in UAE – Uses, Benefits & Applications

Introduction

Defoamers are chemical additives used to reduce and control foam formation in industrial processes. HA International Chemicals Trading LLC supplies high-performance defoamers across the UAE.

What is a Defoamer?

Defoamers, also known as anti-foaming agents, are substances that break down foam and prevent its formation in liquids.

Applications

  • Water treatment
  • Paints and coatings
  • Food processing
  • Oil and gas industry
  • Chemical manufacturing
