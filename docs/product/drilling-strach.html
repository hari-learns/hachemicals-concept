Drilling Strach

Drilling Starch — Fluid Loss Control Agent for Drilling Fluids

Supplied by HA International Chemicals Trading LLC, a chemical supplier based in the UAE. Drilling Starch is a modified natural polymer used as a fluid loss control agent in both freshwater and brine-based drilling fluids. It enhances filter cake quality, improves mud rheology, and performs effectively under a wide range of temperatures and salinity conditions.

Key Benefits

  • Effective Fluid Loss Control — Reduces filtrate loss into the formation, protecting well integrity.
  • Improved Filter Cake Quality — Produces a thin, low-permeability filter cake for better wellbore stability.
  • Enhanced Mud Rheology — Improves flow properties and viscosity control of drilling fluids.
  • Wide Compatibility — Performs effectively across a range of temperatures and salinity conditions, including brine-based systems.

Key Applications

  • Onshore & Offshore Drilling — Suitable for a wide range of well environments.
  • Freshwater & Brine-Based Mud Systems — Compatible with multiple drilling fluid formulations.
  • High-Salinity Well Conditions — Maintains performance where salinity control is critical.
