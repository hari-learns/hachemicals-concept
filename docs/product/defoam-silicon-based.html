Key Benefits

  • Rapid Foam Suppression — Breaks down surface and entrained foam quickly and effectively.
  • Wide Performance Range — Delivers superior anti-foaming performance across a wide range of temperatures and pH levels.

Key Applications

  • Wastewater Treatment — Controls foam during aeration and processing.
  • Industrial Manufacturing — Manages foam in chemical processing systems.
