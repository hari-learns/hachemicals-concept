    Ammonium Chloride is a white crystalline salt highly soluble in water, commonly used across various industrial, agricultural, and laboratory applications. Our high-purity Ammonium Chloride is ideal for use in metalwork, batteries, fertilizers, and as a flux in electroplating and soldering processes.

    Key Features:

    • Chemical Formula: NH₄Cl

    • Appearance: White crystalline powder

    • Purity: 99% min (Industrial Grade)

    • Odor: Odorless

    • Solubility: Highly soluble in water

    • Packaging: Available in 25 kg bags or custom packaging on request

    • Storage & Handling:
      Store in a cool, dry, and well-ventilated area. Keep the container tightly closed and away from incompatible substances like strong acids or alkalis.
