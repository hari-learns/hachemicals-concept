VFD MD290T1.5/2.2PB-INT

The VFD MD290T1.5/2.2PB-INT is a high-performance variable frequency drive (VFD) designed for reliable and energy-efficient control of three-phase induction motors. Ideal for a wide range of industrial and commercial applications, the MD290 series offers advanced motor control, enhanced durability, and intelligent features for improved automation and system optimization.
With its compact design, intuitive operation, and superior heat dissipation, the VFD MD290T1.5/2.2PB-INT is perfect for systems requiring stable speed control, torque performance, and energy savings.

⚙️ Key Features:

  • Model: MD290T1.5/2.2PB-INT

  • Input Voltage: 3-phase 380~480V AC

  • Output Power:

    • 1.5 kW (Normal Duty)

    • 2.2 kW (Heavy Duty)

  • Output Frequency: 0.1–599 Hz

  • Control Mode: V/F and sensorless vector control

  • Built-in Brake Chopper: For faster motor stop response

  • Communication Interface: RS-485 (Modbus RTU)

  • Intelligent Functions: PID control, multi-speed operation, energy-saving mode

  • EMC Filter: Built-in for reduced electrical noise

  • Protective Features:

    • Overvoltage & undervoltage protection

    • Overcurrent protection

    • Overheating and phase loss protection

  • Operating Temperature: -10°C to 50°C

  • Compact Design: Space-saving and easy to install

  • User-Friendly Interface: LED keypad with parameter copy function
