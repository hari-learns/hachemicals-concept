Microsilica Supplier in UAE – Uses, Benefits & Applications

Introduction

Microsilica, also known as silica fume, is a highly reactive material used to enhance the strength and durability of concrete. HA International Chemicals Trading LLC supplies premium-grade microsilica across the UAE.

What is Microsilica?

Microsilica is an ultrafine powder collected as a by-product of silicon metal production. It is rich in silicon dioxide (SiO₂) and is widely used in high-performance concrete.

Applications

  • High-strength concrete
  • Shotcrete and tunneling
  • Precast elements
  • Marine structures
  • Oil well cementing

Benefits

  • Increases compressive strength
  • Reduces permeability
  • Improves resistance to chemicals
  • Enhances long-term durability
